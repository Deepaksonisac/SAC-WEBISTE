The 9th OIL INDIA Gold Cup Football Tournament 2026 culminated yesterday with Punjab FC emerging as Champions, defeating Oil India FC in a penalty shootout after a gripping 1–1 draw following extra time in the much-awaited Final at the Nehru Maidan Sports Complex, Duliajan.

The grand finale was preceded by a vibrant Closing Ceremony, graced by Shri Terash Gowalla, Hon’ble Member of Parliament, Rajya Sabha, as Chief Guest; Shri Sanjay Verma, Resident Chief Executive, OIL, and ...Shri Taranga Gogoi, Hon’ble MLA, Naharkatia LAC, as Guests of Honour, along with other special guests and officials from OIL. The ceremony featured addresses by the dignitaries and cultural performances by students of the Duliajan Club Music Academy and renowned singer Ms. Dipanwita Deka.

On the field, Akrang Narzary (Jersey No. 15) put Oil India FC ahead in the 33rd minute before Yendrembam Boby Singh (Jersey No. 25) equalised for Punjab FC in the 61st minute. With the score remaining 1–1 after extra time, Punjab FC prevailed in the ensuing penalty shootout to lift the coveted title, while Oil India FC finished as runners-up.

Final Result: Punjab FC defeated Oil India FC in the penalty shootout after a 1–1 draw following extra time.

Individual Honours:
• Best Player of the Final Match: Singamayum Shamit (Jersey No. 7), Punjab FC
• Best Goalkeeper: Navajyoti Ayan (Jersey No. 21), Oil India FC
• Best Linkman: Aniket Yadav (Jersey No. 6), Punjab FC
• Best Defender: Suraj Murmu (Jersey No. 3), Punjab FC
• Best Player of the Tournament: Rituraj Mohan (Jersey No. 26), Oil India FC

The tournament concluded with the Prize Distribution Ceremony, honouring the champions, runners-up and outstanding performers, bringing another memorable edition of the OIL INDIA Gold Cup to a close with a celebration of sporting excellence, teamwork, camaraderie and the enduring spirit of football.

As part of Swachhata Hi Seva 2026, Oil India Limited carried forward the spirit of a cleaner and greener environment through plantation drives at multiple locations in Tinsukia.

Under OIL LAKSHYA, a CSR initiative of Oil India Limited, plantation drives were organised at Janamukh Lezaihula Anganwadi Centre and Tinsukia Commerce College (Small Khelo India Football Centre) in collaboration with the Small Khelo India Centre (SKIC), Tinsukia.

Players, coaches, guardians, children..., senior citizens and members of the local community joined the initiative, reinforcing the importance of tree plantation, environmental protection and community participation in keeping our surroundings clean and green.

The initiative continued at Bokuloni Chichiya Playground, Bhadoi Panchali, where around 100 saplings were planted in and around the playground in the presence of senior citizens and members of the local community.

Through OIL LAKSHYA, Oil India Limited continues to encourage community participation in building cleaner, greener and more sustainable surroundings.
